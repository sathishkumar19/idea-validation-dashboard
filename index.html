Key Insights

Biggest score divergence

Sports Facility scored 23/35 with Claude but only 13/35 for founders — it costs ₹70L–1Cr to build, more than the entire budget, needs no tech skills, and only one founder can run it.

Pivot flips the verdict

Claude rated Scrappy "Kill it" (14/35). The ITAD pivot — certified laptop and server disposal for DPDP — scores 24/35 for founders. The buyer is an IT peer; deal size is ₹50k–5L.

Budget discipline

Spend at most ₹3L proving demand before committing to software. The Compliance SaaS earns revenue first (₹75k gap reports, manual delivery) and funds its own build from there.

Remote work split

Top ideas (#3 RegTech and #8 ArivuPalam) let Bangalore own sales and client meetings while Australia and US build the platform async. Sports Facility and clinic field sales give only one founder a meaningful role.

ArivuPalam — Phase 1 wedge

ArivuPalam (28/45) is the team's second testable idea. Founders deliver IT sessions at TN engineering colleges as the first product — no platform needed. The Project VC Circle (expert mentors reviewing student intern projects like VCs) is genuinely novel and differentiates from TCS iON and Naan Mudhalvan.
